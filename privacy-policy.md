# BinaBalance Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 17 September 2026**

BinaBalance respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the BinaBalance mobile application.

## Data Collection

BinaBalance does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

Information entered while using the app, including eating-urge ratings, selected triggers, sound-session choices, and session history, is stored locally on the user's device.

BinaBalance does not currently require users to create an account and does not use advertising or third-party analytics services.

## Local Data

BinaBalance stores certain information locally on your device so that features such as session history, progress, and app preferences can function.

This locally stored information is not transmitted to the developer.

Removing the app may remove locally stored app data, depending on your device and backup settings.

## Age Requirement

BinaBalance is intended for adults aged 18 years and over.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if BinaBalance's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was last updated.

## Contact

For questions about BinaBalance or this Privacy Policy, please use the contact information provided on the BinaBalance Support page.
