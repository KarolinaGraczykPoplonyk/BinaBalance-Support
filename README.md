# BinaBalance Support

Welcome to the official support page for BinaBalance.

## About BinaBalance

BinaBalance is a sound-based wellbeing app designed to help adults pause, reflect on eating urges, and observe changes in their self-reported experience before and after a sound session.

## How to use BinaBalance

1. Rate your current urge to eat.
2. Select what feels closest to what is driving the urge.
3. Choose an immersive eating or nature sound.
4. Select a session duration and listen using headphones.
5. Rate your urge again after the session.
6. View your results, progress and insights.

## Important Information

BinaBalance is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

BinaBalance is a wellbeing and self-reflection tool. It is not designed to suppress physical hunger, replace meals, support restrictive eating, diagnose or treat any medical condition, or replace professional medical advice.

If you have concerns about your eating, appetite, or health, please seek advice from an appropriate healthcare professional.

## Privacy

BinaBalance does not require users to create an account or sign in.
